## **PROJECT CHARTER – Team #1**

1.  **PURPOSE / BACKGROUND:** *(why project being undertaken; problem/need being addressed)*

Our client, Evan Holt, would like to address the following issues:

-   New hikers struggle to find accurate, centralized, and up-to-date trail information
-   Popular trails are overcrowded, lesser-known trails are underutilized
-   Websites utilized by PNW hikers are outdated, difficult to navigate, or focused mainly on advertising gear – rather than hiking safety and stewardship

1.  **OBJECTIVE:** *(what project aims to achieve, specific and measurable)*

Our client would like to create a centralized web application that:

-   Is a “trusted authority” on PNW hiking
-   Allows our client’s organization, The Great Outdoors, to better serve PNW hikers and grow as an organization

The web application would include the following features:

User accounts:

-   Trail reviews and ratings
-   Guided hike registrations
-   Donation or membership support
-   Eventually – partner w/ local parks, nonprofits, & outdoor organizations

1.  **SCOPE:**

In-scope:

-   Create individual user accounts
-   View 10 trails total
-   View: reviews, trail details, & membership information

Trail details include:

-   -   Trail name
    -   Location
    -   Distance
    -   Elevation gain
    -   Difficulty level
    -   Trail type or route
    -   Safety and seasonal notes
    -   Environmental stewardship information
    -   Key highlights/features
    -   Optional: community engagement information
-   Compose freeform/written reviews of trails

Out-of-scope:

-   Interactive maps / GPS tracking
-   Communication between users / embedded messaging system

1.  **HIGH-LEVEL DELIVERABLES:** *(key outputs/products of the project)*

*Deliverable #1:* UI for viewing trail information, including trail reviews

*Deliverable #2:* UI for users to create trail reviews

*Deliverable #3:* User-friendly guided hike registration system
